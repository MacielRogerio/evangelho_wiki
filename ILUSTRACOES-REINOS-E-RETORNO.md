# Ilustrações da Linha do Tempo

Expansão após a dedicação do Primeiro Templo. As 60 imagens novas foram produzidas com a ferramenta integrada de geração e convertidas para WebP de 1000 × 500 px. As cenas são reconstruções artísticas ou, nas mensagens poéticas, ilustrações simbólicas.

## Instruções de geração

A. Illustration for Evangelho.wiki. [Scene]. Historical realism in a detailed graphic novel, defined brush contours and delicate hatching, natural anatomy with one right and one left hand, detailed material textures, cinematic depth, ochre, terracotta, olive and blue in warm light. Balanced panoramic crop-safe 2:1 scene, exactly one period, no readable inscriptions, captions, logos, watermarks, anachronisms, grotesque anatomy, physical form of God or explicit violence.

B. Evangelho.wiki illustrated biblical chronology. [Scene]. Only one coherent scene, panoramic 2:1 crop-safe frame, realistic high detail graphic-novel illustration, delicate hatching, gold light with ochre, olive, blue and terracotta, natural faces and anatomically plausible hands. No anachronisms, captions, physical depiction of God, overt violence, watermark or logos.

C. Evangelho.wiki illustrated biblical chronology. [Scene]. One coherent scene, panoramic 2:1, realistic detailed graphic novel, natural hands and faces, ochre and warm lamplight, no modern elements, text, divine figure or watermark.
